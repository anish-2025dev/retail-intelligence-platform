# Five-slide executive presentation

1. **Business problem** - the client's questions and why they matter.
2. **Data and methodology** - sources, pipeline (validate -> dbt -> ML), data-quality results (records rejected and why).
3. **Key insights** - 3 headline findings with one chart each.
4. **Recommendations** - specific actions, owner, and how success will be measured.
5. **Impact and limitations** - what can/can't be concluded (no cost data, observational findings, forecast error), next steps.
